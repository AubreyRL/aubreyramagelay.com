Artist statement

I create because it is my nature rather than a choice. We all project our inner world outward through gesture, language, and image. Working primarily in oil painting, my practice is shaped by contemplative traditions and an ongoing exploration of perception, transformation, and the shifting boundaries of the self.

Through symbolic and mythic structures, I examine the tension between illusion and reality, and the ways identity dissolves into something larger than the individual. Figures and archetypes emerge as vessels for psychological and spiritual processes, reflecting the continuous movement between order and chaos, rupture and renewal.

My paintings suggest that nothing exists in isolation: we are complex knots within a shared fabric of experience. I hope the work invites recognition, reflection, and moments of quiet awakening.
