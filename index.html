Ophic Metamorphosis

2014 – 2024

Experiencing personal growth and transformation can be difficult. Transformation requires a new energy to enter our system, something to break the momentum of our existence. This can be painful, and in fact often comes in the form of an ordeal. Any ordeal can be frightening, but ultimately change is a rewarding experience. This is the heart of the ophic metamorphosis: just as a snake sheds its skin to come out fresh and bright, so too can people transform themselves in ways they never expected.

Change is a powerful thing, and one that is not without dangers, as change is a form of entropy introduced into the system of self. Entropy, by its nature, makes us permeable to influences outside our control, for better or worse. Still, it is only through embracing those influences and incorporating them into our life that we can learn to fully exist within the ever-shifting manifestation that we somehow identify as our self.

This show is like a treasure map. If you let it, it can lead you to the change you have been searching for within yourself.
